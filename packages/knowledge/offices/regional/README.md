- Unknown coordinates, appointment URLs and hours are omitted. Published hours retain appointment restrictions, separate service hours and holiday exceptions where supplied. Finanzamt records state the appointment requirement without inferring opening hours.
- FSW sources use current “Beratung und Service-Center” terminology. These are the service centres requested by the brief.
